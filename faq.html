Questions

The things people ask us most. Don't see yours? Ask us.

What is Decideish?

A restaurant finder that picks one place for you. You answer a few quick questions and get a single recommendation, with directions, instead of a long list to scroll through.

How does it pick a restaurant?

It starts with restaurants of the cuisine you chose, then ranks them by how well they match your budget, your mood and how far you'll go. Cuisines you saved as favorites get a small boost. The best match is shown first.

Why not just use Google Maps?

Google gives you a long list and leaves the choosing to you. Decideish gives you one pick that already fits your mood, budget and diet, and it remembers what you liked. If you give a place a thumbs down, we won't suggest it again for 30 days.

Why only one restaurant?

Because a long list is the problem. Seeing dozens of options and reviews is what makes choosing so tiring. One good pick is faster, and if it's not right, you can ask for a different one.

Is it free?

Yes. The website is free, and the app will be free to download and use.

Do I need an account?

No. You can take the quiz and get a pick without one. An account lets you save your food preferences, see where you've been, and remember the places you liked or want hidden.

Where does Decideish work?

We're starting in Cambridge, MA, because it has so many kinds of food in a small area. More areas will come later.

How do you choose the restaurants?

Our own list comes first: places we've researched ourselves, including local favorites and spots that aren't widely known yet. To cover more cuisines, we also use restaurants listed on OpenStreetMap, a free map built by volunteers. Those we haven't checked ourselves, so we say so on the result and you should confirm hours, prices and menus before you go.

Can I order delivery?

In the app, you'll be able to open the restaurant in your delivery app when it's available there. On the website, you get directions for now.

Is there an app?

It's coming, starting on Android, with iPhone planned for later. Email decideish@yahoo.com for the Android download link. You can use this website in the meantime.

Can I suggest a restaurant or a feature?

Yes, please do. Use our feedback form, or message us on Instagram @decideish. Both go straight to our team.

What about my allergies?

You can add allergies and your diet to your preferences. Always double-check allergens with the restaurant before you order. Restaurants can change their recipes and ingredients.

What happens to my data?

We save your taste profile so your picks fit you. Your data is never sold, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. The details are in our privacy policy.
