What Decideish is

Decideish recommends restaurants based on your answers. A recommendation is a suggestion, not a guarantee about a restaurant's food, service, prices or hours.

Restaurant information

We research every restaurant, but details like hours, prices and menus change. Check with the restaurant before you go, especially for hours.

Allergies and diets

Decideish can't confirm what's in a dish. If you have an allergy or a dietary need, always confirm with the restaurant before you order.
